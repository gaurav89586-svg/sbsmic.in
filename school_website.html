🏫 About Our College

S.B.S. Memorial Inter College, Ghasipura (Muzaffarnagar) ek pratishthit shikshan sansthan hai. Hamara uddeshya vidyarthiyon ko uchha stariya shiksha ke saath-saath moral values (sanskar) pradaan karna hai, taaki ve desh ke zimmedar nagrik ban sakein.
